## Fan

Air Fan Loop by Rvgerxini, CC0 1.0:
https://freesound.org/people/Rvgerxini/sounds/546174/

The official public HQ MP3 preview was used (not the login-required original):
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/546/546174_9453283-hq.mp3

Converted to Vorbis quality 5 after a 50 ms circular equal-power overlap to smooth the repeat boundary. Preview/transcoding quality differs from the original upload. The retained `fan.ogg` was decoded offline and downmixed to mono 44100 Hz signed 16-bit little-endian `fan.pcm` for continuous playback through the app's AudioTrack mixer, without MediaPlayer loop restarts:

```sh
ffmpeg -i fan.ogg -ac 1 -ar 44100 -c:a pcm_s16le -f s16le fan.pcm
```

The raw PCM contains 66963 samples (133926 bytes); the catalog supplies its rate and mono format.

## Preparation and verification

Files are decoded and inspected with ffprobe/ffmpeg for format, duration, sample peaks and numerical loop boundaries. No claim of subjective listening verification or perfectly seamless Android MediaPlayer playback is made. The tablet should be used to audition loop transitions and relative loudness before release.
